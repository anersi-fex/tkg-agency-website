Build your book with The Koka Group

We're a new, growing independent agency in Michigan. If you're a licensed property & casualty producer — or you're ready to become one — we'd like to talk.

01

What you get

Independent · Multi-carrier · Mentored

Independent contracts

You build relationships that are yours. No captive product to push, no quota for a single carrier.

Multiple carriers

Quote the market, not one price sheet. When you can actually find the best fit, closing gets a lot easier.

Real mentorship

Learn from a producer who built a book from a standing start — how to source leads, work referral partners, and quote to bind.

Tools that work

Comparative rating, a CRM, and lead systems set up before your first call. You focus on clients, not spreadsheets.

02

Who we're looking for

  • Licensed Michigan P&C producers — or people willing to get licensed. We'll point you to the course and the exam.
  • People who pick up the phone. Most of this job is conversations: clients, loan officers, realtors, carriers.
  • Referral-minded. You like building relationships with the people who send business — and returning the favor.
  • Honest to a fault. We'd rather lose a sale than put someone in the wrong policy. You should feel the same way.
  • Bilingual is a plus. Michigan is diverse; so are our clients.
03

How it works

  1. 1

    Intro call

    Fifteen minutes. Where you are, what you want to build, and whether we're a fit.

  2. 2

    Meet the team & see the tools

    Sit in on a quote, see the systems, and ask anything.

  3. 3

    Contract & onboarding

    Carrier appointments, systems access, and a first-30-days plan we build together.
